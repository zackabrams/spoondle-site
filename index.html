How to play

  • Place a word from each column onto the mat.
  • Swap a tile between them to uncover an answer.
  • Careful, the answer may read backwards!
  • No luck? Try replacing one of the words.

Compound words are two words joined together. Proper nouns are people, places, and titles. Sneaky splits are ordinary words that break in two.

Hint lights up a tile that wants to swap. Reveal shows whether the tiles you just tried are sticky or swappy. Be competitive with timer mode.

Tip: Try saying the words out loud!
