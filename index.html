How to play

  • Place a word from each column onto the mat.
  • Swap a tile between them to uncover an answer.
  • Careful, the answer may read backwards!
  • No luck? Try replacing one of the words.

Words & phrases: complete words form an answer. Proper nouns: names, places, brands, and titles. Sneaky splits: single words split somewhere surprising.

Hint lights up a tile that wants to swap. Peek shows whether the tiles you just tried are sticky or swappy. Be competitive with timer mode. Switch the mode or theme anytime in Settings.

Tip: Try saying the words out loud!

Inspired by spoonerisms. A game by Zack Abrams.
