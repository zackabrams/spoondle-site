How to play

  1. Place a word from each column onto the mat.
  2. Drag tiles around until you uncover an answer.
  3. Careful, it may be backwards!

Compound words are two words joined together. Proper nouns are people, places, and titles. Hidden words are ordinary words that split in two.

Use the "Hint" button to reveal a tile that wants to swap. "Show a clue" will tell you if the tiles you chose are sticky or swappy.

Have fun!
